# The Bound Identity — round 25

Unicode Seal adds independently retained digest checking and explicit engine and
runtime identity to the existing replay workflow. Its README includes the demo,
portable handoff contract and limitations. Goal and ancestral tools are unchanged.

The installed image connector generated the pigment; local compositing added only
that pigment to bare rock. This was a generation-and-composite workflow, not an
image edit API. It preserves all pixels outside the added pigment. Only the final
wall and its required preview remain as new pictures. The current wall alias and
the separately named upload output refer to the same completed image.

Prompt used with the image connector:

> A single tiny prehistoric cave-painting motif, centered on a plain pure white
> background for compositing onto an existing cave wall. Two irregular red ochre
> pebbles bound together by one loose charcoal-black enclosing loop, with a small
> yellow ochre dab at the loop's join. Hand-painted with worn earth pigment and
> rough broken burnt-stick strokes, humble Lascaux style, asymmetrical, flat
> pigment, no shadows, no 3D. Motif occupies central half of square, everything
> else pure white. No text, no letters, no numbers, no border, no hands, no people,
> no frog; this is only a small additional mark for a wall that already depicts
> Pepe. Only red/yellow ochre and charcoal pigments.

The white backdrop was removed in memory using a luminance mask. The cropped
pigment was resized to 110 × 82 and alpha-composited at (956,830), at a maximum
opacity of 190/255 so underlying rock remains visible. Final mark is subdued;
its small yellow join and broken charcoal texture lose detail in the preview.
No visual requirement was observed unmet in local review; this is not independent
visual approval. The inherited Pepe and both five-digit hands are untouched.
